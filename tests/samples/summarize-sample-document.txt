EXPLANATION OF BENEFITS — SAMPLE NOTICE

This is not a bill. This notice summarizes how your health plan processed a recent claim, how benefits were applied, and how to understand the amounts shown below.

Member name: Sample Member
Plan name: River Valley Select PPO
Group: Demonstration group (sample only)

Date of service: March 15, 2024
Claim received: March 18, 2024
Processed: March 22, 2024

Provider: Westside Family Clinic (in-network primary care)
Procedure: Annual wellness visit — preventive care

The provider billed four hundred twenty dollars. Because this office participates in your plan’s network, discounts apply before benefits are calculated. Your plan’s allowed amount for this service was three hundred ten dollars.

This visit is categorized as preventive care under your plan. For many members, preventive services are covered in full when performed by an in-network provider and meet plan criteria. After applying your plan’s rules, the plan paid two hundred forty-eight dollars toward covered charges. The remaining portion of the allowed amount may reflect a deductible you are still meeting, or other plan-specific adjustments described in your benefit booklet.

You may owe additional amounts such as a copayment or coinsurance, depending on your benefits and whether you have satisfied your annual deductible. Those balances will appear on a separate statement from the provider if applicable.

Amounts shown here reflect the plan’s determination at the time of processing. If other claims for the same time period are still pending, totals may change when those claims finalize.

If you disagree with how this claim was processed, you may ask for an internal review as described in your member materials. You may also have the right to an external review under state or federal rules for certain disputes.

For general questions about terminology on this notice, refer to the definitions section of your handbook. For account-specific questions, use the contact options printed on your member identification card. Keep this notice with your health records for your reference.
